## Corrections verified

- Human review is bound to the current product evidence and restrictions. Changed
  ingredients or restrictions invalidate reuse and require review to restart.
- Non-finite or out-of-range model confidence cannot establish safety.
- Nutrition coverage includes custom carbohydrate, fat and fibre minimums.
- Incomplete retailer totals cannot produce misleading savings percentages or AI savings claims.
- Ambiguous basket item names request clarification; invalid compound edits do not partially apply.
- Buying-time advice excludes all perishable products.
- Negotiation supports an exact meal count and partial final day, carried into confirmed
  constraints, optimisation and meal planning. Solver timeouts do not establish maximum coverage.
- Confirmed dislikes filter substitution candidates; private preference fields and intake/routing
  payloads are omitted from oversight audit payloads, including legacy reads. Forgetting also
  sanitises stored payloads; historical confirmed shopping facts are retained.
- “No allergies” is not interpreted as a food dislike. Household save feedback reflects immediate changes.

Privacy redaction, stale review evidence, malformed confidence, custom nutrition targets,
ambiguous edits and incomplete price comparisons have automated regressions; not every
one was independently induced through the browser. The last audit-redaction change was
covered by the final tests; the earlier-running browser server was not restarted for that
last change, so the browser audit screenshot is evidence of traceability, not of that fix.

## Live provider limitation

The existing Groq configuration (`openai_compatible`, `qwen/qwen3.8-27b`) was attempted.
Calls returned **HTTP 403: “Access denied. Please check your network settings.”** A direct
models request outside the execution sandbox also returned 403. This does not establish
whether the configured model is available to this account. No live inference passed.

The application correctly surfaced degraded operation and continued with labelled offline
fallback. The browser results above therefore validate fallback operation and deterministic
workflows, **not live Groq model quality**. A successful live call and corresponding end-to-end
journey still need rerunning once provider/network access is restored. No secret is included
in this report or its screenshots.
